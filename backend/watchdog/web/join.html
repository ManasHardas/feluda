Neighborhood Watchdog

A private, invitation-only network that watches for unusual Wi-Fi disruptions across your neighborhood.

You are invited to join

The app on your phone notices when your home Wi-Fi drops. If several nearby homes report the same thing at about the same time, everyone taking part gets an alert so they can check their cameras and alarms.

This is an anomaly detector. A phone app cannot prove that someone is deliberately blocking radio signals, and it does not replace your alarm system.

How to join

  1. Install the app on your phone. Install on Android Install on iPhone (TestFlight)

    Your neighborhood organizer will send you the install link for your phone.

  2. Come back to this page and tap the button below. The app opens and asks for your street. That is all: there is no account, no e-mail address and no password. Open in the app

    If the button does nothing, open the app and paste your invitation link there. Copy the invitation link Copied.

  • Android: open the install link, accept the tester invitation, and install. You may be asked to allow installs from the testing app.
  • iPhone: install Apple’s TestFlight app first, then open the install link and tap Install.

What is and is not collected

  • Collected: your street, a house number only if you choose to give one, and connectivity events from your phone: when Wi-Fi, internet or cellular service was lost or restored.
  • Never collected: your name, e-mail address or phone number, browsing history, Wi-Fi or router passwords, GPS location, camera footage, microphone audio, messages, or logins for your camera, alarm or internet provider.
  • Other residents never see your address. They see only counts such as “4 households affected”.
  • You can switch monitoring off or leave at any time in the app. Leaving deletes what your phone reported.
