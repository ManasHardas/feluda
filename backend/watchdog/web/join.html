Neighborhood Watchdog

A private, invitation-only network that watches for unusual Wi-Fi disruptions across your neighborhood.

You are invited to join

The app on your phone notices when your home Wi-Fi drops. If several nearby homes report the same thing at about the same time, everyone taking part gets an alert so they can check their cameras and alarms.

This is an anomaly detector. A phone app cannot prove that someone is deliberately blocking radio signals, and it does not replace your alarm system.

What is and is not collected

  • Collected: your house number and street (to confirm you live in this neighborhood), an e-mail address for sign-in, and connectivity events from your phone: when Wi-Fi, internet or cellular service was lost or restored.
  • Never collected: browsing history, Wi-Fi or router passwords, GPS location, camera footage, microphone audio, messages, or logins for your camera, alarm or internet provider.
  • Other residents never see your address. They see only counts such as “4 households affected”.
  • You can switch monitoring off, leave, or delete your account at any time in the app.

Installing

  • Android: open the link, accept the tester invitation, and install. You may be asked to allow installs from the testing app.
  • iPhone: install Apple’s TestFlight app first, then open the link and tap Install.
  • Open Neighborhood Watchdog, read the short introduction, and sign in with your e-mail address.
